Web Development Bootcamps Comparison

By Tarun Sharma • June 28, 2024

Choosing the right web development bootcamp is a key decision in advancing your career as a developer. Here's a detailed comparison of top bootcamps:

1. Le Wagon

Le Wagon is a highly-rated bootcamp known for its intensive full-stack development curriculum. It combines frontend and backend technologies to help you become a proficient web developer. Learn the skills that are most in demand for today's web development job market.

  • Curriculum: Full-stack development, Ruby on Rails, JavaScript, React, SQL.
  • Duration: 12 weeks (Full-time), 24 weeks (Part-time)
  • Cost: $8,000
  • Outcome: 85% of students secure jobs within 6 months of graduation.

2. General Assembly

General Assembly is a global leader in tech education offering an immersive software engineering bootcamp. It focuses on frontend and backend development using modern frameworks and tools, preparing students for dynamic careers in tech.

  • Curriculum: Frontend frameworks, JavaScript, React, APIs, Node.js.
  • Duration: 10 weeks (Full-time)
  • Cost: $7,500
  • Outcome: 90% job placement rate.

3. App Academy

App Academy is famous for its "Deferred Tuition" model, where you pay tuition only after getting a job. It provides rigorous training in data structures, algorithms, and full-stack development, ensuring students are ready for technical interviews and real-world challenges.

  • Curriculum: Data structures, algorithms, Ruby, SQL, DevOps basics, JavaScript.
  • Duration: 16 weeks (Full-time)
  • Cost: $9,000
  • Outcome: 80% job placement rate within 3 months.

Comparison Table

Bootcamp Name Curriculum Duration Cost Job Placement Rate
Le Wagon Full-stack, Ruby on Rails, JavaScript 12 weeks $8,000 85%

Conclusion

Selecting the best bootcamp depends on your learning style, career goals, and budget. Consider factors like the curriculum, duration, cost, and job placement rates when making your decision. With the right bootcamp, you can launch your web development career with confidence and take the first step toward your dream job in tech.
